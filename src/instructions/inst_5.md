# 🌌 Game 2 🌌
 In <b>game 2</b>, you will have to decide **which shield has the least resistance** so that you earn the maximum number of points. (see picture on the right). All spaceships are **identicals**, what differs is the **resistance** of their **shield**. That resistance is **signaled** by the exact **texture** of the shield. shields can be weaker (or stronger) as their texture vary from full <b>white</b> to full <b>black</b>.
 Said differently, the texture of a shield **signals** its **probability** of being **destroyed** by your shot.  When you **succeed** at destroying a shield, you will earn **500 points** - focus on **which texture** it was so that you can maximize your points. When you **fail** 
 at destroying a shield (it deflects the blast) you will get **0 points**. 

The weakest can be equally on the left or on the right, there is no optimal side.
