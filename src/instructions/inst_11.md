# Game 5
<br>
Well done! 🥳
<br>

Now let's continue with **game 5**. In this phase, to maximize your overall score, you need to decide on both shield resistance and average spaceship rewards. Unlike game 4, when a shield deflects the attack you will **receive 0 points**. You can reasonably expect a score of at least **~40000 points**. This phase lasts around ~10min. <br><br>

You will face waves of spaceship pairs. 
In order to destroy a target, you have to press either **left or right arrow key**, and then press **space to shoot**.

## Ready? 

If you click on next, the **fifth game** will start!
